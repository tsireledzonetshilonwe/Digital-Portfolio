About

Background

I graduated from Cape Peninsula University of Technology with an ICT Applications Development qualification (NQF Level 6), and I'm now working as a Software Development Intern at Plum Systems, based between Gauteng and Cape Town.

My day-to-day work sits mostly in Java and Spring Boot on the backend and React, Redux and TypeScript on the front end, backed by SQL and REST APIs. I'm comfortable across the stack, but I care most about the parts where correctness and security actually matter — validating input server-side rather than trusting the client, for instance.

Outside of client work, I'm studying toward the Microsoft AI-200 certification, following an AI Fluency track with ongoing portfolio assignments, and keeping up a personal interest in cybersecurity and OSINT — WHOIS and DNS investigation, Google dorking, Shodan, and cryptographic protocols.
