### ✍️ Reflection (STAR Technique)

**⭐ Situation:**  
In preparation for entering the job market, I participated in an **Interview Skills program** aimed at improving how I communicate and present myself during professional interviews. The course provided guidance on key interview techniques, including how to handle both structured and spontaneous questions confidently.

**🎯 Task:**  
My objective was to strengthen my ability to answer interview questions clearly and professionally. I wanted to learn how to express my experiences using a structured approach while maintaining a confident and authentic tone.

**⚙️ Action:**  
I completed the interview training activities, practiced common and technical interview questions, and studied various answer frameworks — especially the **STAR method**. I also focused on improving my **body language, tone of voice**, and **eye contact** to make a positive impression.

**✅ Result:**  
After completing this training, I feel significantly more confident and prepared for professional interviews. I can now structure my answers effectively, highlight my achievements, and communicate my skills with clarity and confidence. This experience has also improved my ability to remain calm and professional under pressure.
